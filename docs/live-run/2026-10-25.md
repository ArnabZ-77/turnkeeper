## Findings

- **F1 (blocker, environment).** Payouts get `422 INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` for a 1154.93 pot, three times across two
  circles. Every round 3 payout fails, so step 11 can never run. The Balances reading (4997.98) was about 1.7 h
  stale, so the stale-tolerant gate (`app/engine/rounds.py` around line 662) let the call through. The
  personal-wallet charges all succeeded (10 × $200), so the shortfall is on the business (payer) side.
  **Fix:** top up the sandbox business account balance in the dashboard (and the personal accounts if they run
  low), then rerun steps 10 and 11.
- **F2 (major).** In live mode the invoice QR is never produced. The toolkit's `PayPalClient.post`
  (`site-packages/paypal_agent_toolkit/shared/paypal_client.py:85-89`) calls `response.json()` on PayPal's
  `multipart/form-data` answer, logs a WARNING containing the headers, and returns `{}`. A direct probe confirmed
  that the PNG is in the `name="image"` part as base64 (`iVBORw0KGg…`). `recovery._qr_data_url`
  (`app/engine/recovery.py:1020`) also returns None on the raw multipart text, because its base64 regex picks up
  `text/plain`.
- **F3 (minor).** Steps that made live sandbox calls carry mode `rules`:
  - run_round3 takes the task's agent mode (`app/routes/judge_story.py:127`);
  - execute hardcodes `"rules"` (`app/routes/judge_story.py:262`).
- **F4 (minor).** A failed payout leaves no trace on the board: `payout: null`, `payout_attempts: []`, and the
  story step is `next` with a null summary. The reason is only in a toast and the 409 detail
  (`app/engine/rounds.py:692-704`).
- **F5 (minor).** The error text "Payout failed: INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS" gives no next step, such as "the sandbox
  business balance is too low". `live_round3.py` prints null `paypal_balance` fields on this path.
- **F6 (minor).** The vote summary "Votes 4/4 yes" (`app/routes/judge_story.py:241`) reads as unanimous. It was
  4 of 6 with a quorum of 4.
- **F7 (minor).** "Advance to T-3" reports "+0 d" (`app/routes/judge_story.py:187`) because the fixture already
  starts at T-3.
- **F8 (minor).** run_round3 takes 27 s: charges and confirmations run one after another.
- **F9 (minor).** The forecast reason says "next 2027-01-08" while Ravi's reply commits to Friday 2027-01-01. The
  inbound reply also has `round_n: null`.
