# Organizer interview script (6 questions, ~15 minutes)

Purpose: real evidence for the Potential Impact criterion and the baseline for the in-app counters. Talk to 2–4 people
who organise or belong to a rotating savings circle (chit, committee/kameti, susu, tanda, chama, stokvel).

Before recording anything, read the consent line and get a "yes" in writing (chat message is fine):

> "I'm building a hackathon project about savings circles. May I quote what you tell me, by first name or
> anonymously, in a public demo video and write-up? You can withdraw any time before Nov 12."

Keep a copy of the consent for every quote used. If no written consent, use the insight but not the quote.

## Questions

1. **Shape of the circle.** How many members, how much per round, how often, and how is the payout order decided?
2. **Collection.** How do contributions actually arrive (cash, UPI/PayPal/Venmo/Zelle, bank transfer)? Who chases late ones, and how?
3. **Baseline time (ask precisely).** For the *last completed round*: from the first reminder to the pot being paid,
   roughly how many minutes did you spend on circle admin? How many messages did you send about it?
   → record: `minutes_per_round = ___`, `messages_per_round = ___`
4. **Shortfalls.** What happened the last time someone was short or late? Who covered it, and what did it cost the group?
5. **Trust.** Has anyone ever taken an early pot and stopped paying? What do you do today to prevent that?
6. **Would-you-use.** If each member approved once on PayPal and an assistant collected, chased, and paid out
   automatically, what would worry you, and what would make you try it?

## After the call

- Write the numbers from Q3 into `docs/sources.md` under "Interview baseline" (first name or pseudonym, date, consent: yes/no).
- Pull one quotable sentence per person (max 20 words) for the video's opening beat.
- Note any remedy they mentioned that is not in our remedy menu (retry date, turn swap, buffer advance, repay by invoice).
