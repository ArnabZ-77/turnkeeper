# Recorded AI takes, 3 Nov 2026 (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, prompt v2)

What ships: `app/fixtures/llm_cache.jsonl`, 68 recorded model turns, all `gemini/gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. That is the
model the held-out remedy eval (16/16) was measured on. A deploy with no key replays these turns and labels
them "AI replay (Gemini, recorded)"; a miss falls back to the labelled rules mode.

How they were made: `scripts/record_ai_takes.py` against a local server on the **live PayPal sandbox** with
`TK_LLM_CACHE=auto`, `TK_LLM_MIN_INTERVAL=4` (spacing for the free tier), then `scripts/export_llm_cache.py`
(68 written, 0 skipped by its email/key/PayPal-id filter).

## Failed attempts, logged

- 14:46 to 14:57 IST: 9 × HTTP 429 from the free tier on bursts. Two steps fell back to rules mode. This led to
  the opt-in pacing (`TK_LLM_MIN_INTERVAL`, off by default: the demo still drops to rules at once on a 429).
- 15:40 to 15:43 IST: repeated `APITimeoutError` and one 503 from Gemini, a provider-side outage. Retried at 15:50.
- Replay misses found and fixed before the final recording:
  - `_RECOVERY_ID_RE` in `app/agent/llm.py` contained literal backspace characters instead of word boundaries, so
    remedy ids in prose were never normalized.
  - The live PayPal balance and its age ("4.4 h old") entered the treasurer's context. They are now always
    normalized, including the `null` value right after a restart.

compile, forecast, Round 3, recovery, the s06 recovery, and Story B consents and votes were all `replay`, 12 of 12,
in both runs.
