# Prior art

Rotating savings circles are old and widespread, and people have built software for them before. Turnkeeper does not
claim to have invented digital circles. Its claim is narrower: an **agent treasurer** that collects, recovers and
pays out on PayPal rails, under the circle's own frozen rules. Every figure below is taken from
[`docs/sources.md`](sources.md), with its wording and source; nothing else is cited.

| Prior art | What it is | Figure (source) | How Turnkeeper differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mission Asset Fund (MAF) Lending Circles** | Formal, nonprofit-run lending circles in the US | "Formal lending circles: 99% repaid, +168 credit points — MAF / SFSU study" ([MAF](https://www.missionassetfund.org/lending-circles/), [ScienceDaily on the SFSU study](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2013/06/130604153513.htm), [JPMorgan Chase](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/newsroom/stories/expanding-financial-access)) | Cited only as evidence that formal circles help, **never as Turnkeeper's outcome**. Turnkeeper makes no credit-building claim. |
| **MoneyFellows** | A ROSCA app in Egypt | "MoneyFellows: 8.5M+ users, profitable in Egypt — TechCrunch, 2025" ([TechCrunch 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/04/moneyfellows-raises-13m-to-take-its-group-savings-model-outside-egypt), [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/31/money-fellows-an-egyptian-fintech-digitizing-money-circles-raises-31m-funding)) | ROSCA apps run the circle as a product with their own rails. Turnkeeper is a treasurer agent for a circle that keeps its own rules, with collection and payout on PayPal. |
| **PayPal Money Pools and money pooling** | PayPal's group-collection features | "PayPal retired Money Pools in 2021 and relaunched money pooling in Nov 2024" ([PayPal UK newsroom, 2021](https://newsroom.uk.paypal-corp.com/evolving-the-ways-you-can-collect-and-raise-money), [PayPal newsroom, 14 Nov 2024](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2024-11-14-PayPal-Unveils-Innovative-Money-Pooling-Feature,-Simplifying-Group-Expenses-Between-Family-and-Friends)) | A pool collects for a group purchase. A rotating circle collects every round and pays a different member each time, so it needs consent once, recurring collection, shortfall handling and payouts. |
